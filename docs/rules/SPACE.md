# Space, Territory and Bioload Rules

Status: `ACCEPTED`

## AQ-SPACE-001 — Recommended tank size is not an automatic current diagnosis

A species recommendation such as minimum volume or tank length is a planning prior unless it is explicitly classified as a hard physical constraint.

For an existing aquarium, being below a recommendation must not by itself generate a claim that the current tank is already overloaded, unsafe, or requires immediate livestock removal.

Current action must also consider actual tank context and observed evidence.

## AQ-SPACE-002 — Separate physical space, territory and bioload

AquaGuide must not collapse all “space” concerns into one score.

Treat at least these dimensions separately:

- **Physical space** — footprint, length, swimming room and other physical constraints;
- **Territory pressure** — defended zones, aggression, breeding defense, hiding/access pressure;
- **Bioload pressure** — waste/feeding/filtration-related biological load.

A warning in one dimension does not automatically prove failure in the others.

## AQ-SPACE-003 — Temperament must not inflate bioload

Behavior labels such as `Aggressive` or `Territorial` must not increase a species' bioload through an arbitrary multiplier.

Behavioral risk belongs in aggression/territory logic. Bioload must be based on inputs that actually describe biological load and environmental processing capacity.

## AQ-SPACE-004 — Avoid false precision

Do not expose exact-looking load percentages or liter requirements as authoritative current-tank truth unless the inputs and model support that precision.

When the data is heuristic, present the result as a qualitative prior/pressure signal and expose the relevant uncertainty.
